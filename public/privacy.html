Privacy statement

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Information stored by this app

The Dice Vault does not require an account and does not send your saved actions or roll-to-hit modifier to an app server. These items are stored in local storage in the browser you are using. They stay on that browser and device unless you clear its site data. They are not synced between browsers or devices. Anyone with access to the same browser profile may also be able to access them.

When you import an actions file, the file is read in your browser and its contents replace your saved list after you confirm. When you export, the browser downloads a JSON file to your device. The app does not upload either file.

Service providers

This website is hosted by Cloudflare. When your browser requests the site, Cloudflare may process technical request information such as your IP address, browser details, and requested pages to deliver and protect the service. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google to retrieve those font files, which may provide Google with technical information such as your IP address. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads based on your visits to this site or other sites. These technologies may collect or receive information such as your IP address, browser or device information, and interactions with ads. Google's advertising cookies may be used by Google and its partners for these purposes.

You can manage personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. You can also visit YourAdChoices to learn about opting out of some participating companies' interest-based advertising. Opting out does not necessarily stop ads from appearing; ads may be less relevant.

Depending on the ad settings for this site, other third-party ad providers may also use cookies or similar technologies. For visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Google requires publishers to obtain consent for certain cookies and personalized advertising uses where applicable. Your choices may be presented through the site's consent message, if shown.

The app does not currently use a separate analytics service. Hosting and font providers may process technical information as described above and in their own privacy policies.
